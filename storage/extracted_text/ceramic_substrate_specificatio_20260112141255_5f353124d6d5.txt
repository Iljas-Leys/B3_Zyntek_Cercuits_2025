Document Title: Ceramic Substrate Prototype Specification Client Use Case The client requires a ceramic-based PCB prototype suitable for high-temperature operation in a power electronics module. Key Requirements - Operating temperature: up to 220°C - Continuous current: 30A - Copper thickness: 300 µm - Substrate material: Aluminum Nitride (preferred) - Dielectric strength: minimum 20 kV/mm Mechanical Constraints - Board dimensions: 80 mm x 60 mm - Maximum warpage: < 50 µm - Mounting holes: 4x M3 Prototyping Notes Low-volume prototyping (5–10 units) is required for initial validation. Design files will be supplied as Gerber and DXF formats. Open Questions - Feasibility of via-in-ceramic technology - Lead time for AlN substrate sourcing - Cost implications for small batch production
